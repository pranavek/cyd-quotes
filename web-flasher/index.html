CYD Quote Viewer

Flash the firmware directly from your browser.
Requires Chrome or Edge with WebSerial support.

  1. Connect your CYD to your PC via USB.
  2. Click Install above.
  3. Select the correct COM port when prompted.
  4. Wait for flashing to complete (~30 s).
  5. The CYD will reboot and show a WiFi setup screen.
  6. Connect to QuoteViewer-AP with your phone.
  7. Enter your WiFi credentials in the captive portal.
  8. Enjoy your quotes!

Firmware is built automatically from the source repository on every push to main.
